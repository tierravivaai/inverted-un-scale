Recognising ocean and terrestrial stewardship

SOSAC: Small Island Developing States Ocean Stewardship Allocation Component (recognises greater ocean stewardship responsibilities and structural constraints of UN recognised SIDS).

TSAC: Terrestrial Stewardship Allocation Component (recognises greater stewardship responsibilities associated with size, not species richness).

The components divide the selected fund; they are not added on top. SOSAC is shared equally among 39 SIDS. TSAC follows each Party’s share of eligible land area.

Calculation & assumptions

Allocation per Party = fund × band weight ÷ sum of (Party count × weight). Counts: 31, 59, 30, 18, 3, 1. Every Party in a band receives the same allocation. Band totals are the per-Party allocation multiplied by that band’s membership.

The baseline matches Table 1 in iusaf_paper-07052026.docx. The slider interpolation is an exploratory rule introduced for this application, not a formula specified in the paper. The slider uses wᵢ(r) = 0.4 × (baseline wᵢ / 0.4)ln(r) / ln(3.75). This preserves the baseline shape and makes the endpoint ratio exactly r. At 1:1 all weights are 0.4; at 3.75:1 they match the supplied baseline. Moving the slider replaces manual edits.

Strict monotonicity means A₁ > A₂ > A₃ > A₄ > A₅ > A₆. Amber marks an equal adjacent pair; red marks a rising pair. Checks use unrounded weights, so displayed rounded amounts can look equal even when a small difference exists. Whole-dollar displays may differ slightly from the fund when summed due to rounding.
