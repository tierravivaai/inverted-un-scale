# Stewardship follow-on figure

Four panels preserve the exact country order and band colours of country-distributions.png. All share the 0–1.05% vertical scale. Bars show total combined allocations; the dashed line is pure IUSAF, not the reduced IUSAF component inside each scenario.

Ocean-only: TSAC 0%, SOSAC 3%, IUSAF 97%. Computed as 0.97 × pure IUSAF plus 0.03/39 for each eligible SIDS; independently checked against the linear extrapolation of the strict and Gini scenarios. Each receives USD 769,230.77 from SOSAC; net gain subtracts 3% of its previous IUSAF allocation. Strict: TSAC 1.5%, SOSAC 3%, IUSAF 95.5%. Gini minimum: TSAC 2.5%, SOSAC 3%, IUSAF 94.5%. Band-order boundary: TSAC 3%, SOSAC 3%, IUSAF 94%. These are the paper’s recorded scenarios, not newly optimized results.

Band-order boundary: Band 5 mean $5.7040m; China $5.7377m

Band-order boundary at 3% is already marginally across the band-mean threshold, not an exact equality. Country-level rank changes can occur before band means cross. Gini minimum is constrained by the requirement for band-order preservation; it is not the unconstrained optimum. Strict and Gini inputs are country_allocations.csv unrounded shares, joined by ISO3 to the previous figure. Its boundary column does not match the dedicated 3% annex and was not used. The 3% boundary was calculated by linear extrapolation from the 1.5% and 2.5% scenario shares with SOSAC fixed at 3%; all 142 results were verified against the dedicated band-order-boundary annex to its four-decimal million-dollar precision (within USD 51). All scenario shares sum to 100%. Pure IUSAF, SOSAC and TSAC divide the same fixed fund; overlays are not extra money.
